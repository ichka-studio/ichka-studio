About

Studio

Visual worlds where icons, creatures, landscapes and brands become fragments of story.

Imagination and emotion are where everything begins for me. What interests me in an image is the moment it spills over — when contrast, a detail, or a light creates something you cannot quite name.

With AI, I found a terrain that fits me: attempts, intuitions, accidents — and the work behind them, the part you do not see.

Every image I make is a step. Not an endpoint — a step. Something to cross through on the way to the next one.

ICHKA Studio is the space where all of this takes shape: concept art, generative creation and visual storytelling. Worlds to build.

David J.
